# Dashboard page guide

This guide follows the nine-page PDF export supplied with the project.

## Page 1 — Cover
Introduces the Sales Performance & Business Intelligence Dashboard with the tagline “Transforming data into meaningful business insights.”

## Page 2 — Sales Analysis Dashboard
Slicers: year, segment, month, region and category. KPI cards show Sales ($112.878282M), Average Sales (16.86K), Quantity (502K), Order Return (1K), Ship Mode (3), Total Customers (782), Sum of Cost (1M), Profit (79.95M), Regions (4), Total Orders (7K), Expense (75M), Total Categories (3) and Target ($71.083497M). Visuals include regional, monthly and yearly sales; category and sub-category analysis; and top-five products by sales.

## Page 3 — Actual vs Budget Analysis
Shows summary KPIs, a Year/Month/Sales/Target/Difference table, sales by region and category, a sales gauge, and a city-level sales map. The total row displays Sales $112,878,282, Target $71,083,497 and Difference $41,794,785.

## Page 4 — Trend Analysis
Includes orders and customers by year, sales and target by year, sub-category sales, top-ten customers by sales and profit, yearly sales cards, and a daily sales forecast visual. Slicers include year, month and date.

## Page 5 — Detailed Sales Analysis
Includes year/month/date filtering, monthly sales, cash-flow visual, a KPI card, a sales parameter selector (RT Sales, Sales, MTD, QTD, YTD and prior-period measures), and a detailed table with date dimensions and sales measures.

## Page 6 — AI Visual / Key Influencers
The Key Influencers visual asks what influences Sales to increase. The exported view identifies Quarter = Qtr 4 as an influencer and displays an average increase of $59.84K. This is an association reported by the visual, not proof of causation.

## Page 7 — Decomposition Tree
The tree explores Sales through Category, Sub-Category, Region, Segment, Year and Ship Mode. The displayed overall Sales value is $112,878,282.

## Page 8 — Detailed data table
Shows category, sub-category, segments, ship mode, year, quarter, month, day, sales and quantity. The displayed total is Sales $112,878,282 and Quantity 501,669.

## Page 9 — Sales and Profit summary
Shows headline Sales and Profit cards and a sales-by-year donut visual. The exported labels appear inconsistent (each year is shown as 33.33% with the same displayed total); validate this visual's field configuration and measure before interpreting it.

## Validation notes
All figures and interpretations are transcribed from the supplied PDF export. Confirm calculations, field mappings, relationships and model definitions in Power BI Desktop. In particular, validate the year-level donut on page 9 and the daily forecast on page 4 before presenting them as business conclusions.
